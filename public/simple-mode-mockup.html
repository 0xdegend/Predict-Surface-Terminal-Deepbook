UP mint, DOWN coral — the app's real semantic colors, so simple mode reads as Skew, not a bolt-on.
The line is the strike. One fixed price per round (dashed on the chart); "above / below" is up / down against it. No strike picker.
Two things entered, ever: how much, and which way. Cadence (1 / 5 / 60 min) and the confirm are the only other taps.
The round rolls. Countdown + "new round every minute" is the loop; instant trading makes each repeat a single tap.
